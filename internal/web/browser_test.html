Browser Login Setup & Test

Because AutoTTV runs Twitch in an isolated browser window (to allow precise background tracking and automatic closing), it starts with a clean profile that doesn't share your main browser's cookies.

Use this page to perform your initial log in so Twitch remembers your credentials during fully automated watch sessions:

  1. Click Launch Test Browser below.
  2. In the window that opens, sign in manually to your Twitch account.
  3. Once logged in successfully, click Close Test Browser on this dashboard. This gracefully closes the browser, ensuring your cookies are properly saved to disk.
  4. Click Launch Test Browser again to verify that you are automatically logged in!
